Detailed Description

template<typename T, typename Traits>
struct utils::pmr::allocator_base< T, Traits >

A convenience base class providing typedefs for custom allocators.

Remarks
Due to std::allocator_traits, this is not needed most of the time. However, this can be useful when your allocator depends on a common "control type" and you would like to synchronize the typedefs across all your allocators.
