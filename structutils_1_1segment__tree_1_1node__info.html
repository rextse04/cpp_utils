Detailed Description

template<typename Key, typename T, std::copyable Compare = std::less<Key>, std::copyable Sum = std::plus<T>, std::copyable Identity = default_construct<T>, typename KeyContainer = std::vector<Key>, typename MappedContainer = std::vector<T>>
struct utils::segment_tree< Key, T, Compare, Sum, Identity, KeyContainer, MappedContainer >::node_info

Stores properties of the node the traverser is visiting.

Let \(v\) be the subject of the struct, and left and right be its children. Then level is the height of \(v\) (distance of \(v\) to the nearest leaf), and level_idx is the number of nodes preceding \(v\) is its level. *_level and *_idx represent the level and index of left / right, respectively. Level -1 indicates a non-existent node.

[ begin, end ) gives the range of indices of mapped values the sub-tree rooted at \(v\) encompasses, and the sub-trees rooted at the left and right child of the current node encompass [ begin, div_node ) and [ div_node, end ), respectively.
