struct utils\+::segment\+\_\+tree$<$ Key, T, Compare, Sum, Identity, Key\+Container, Mapped\+Container $>$\+::node\+\_\+info}
Stores properties of the node the traverser is visiting. 

Let $v$ be the subject of the struct, and {\ttfamily left} and {\ttfamily right} be its children. Then {\ttfamily level} is the height of $v$ (distance of $v$ to the nearest leaf), and {\ttfamily level\+\_\+idx} is the number of nodes preceding $v$ is its level. {\ttfamily $\ast$\+\_\+level} and {\ttfamily $\ast$\+\_\+idx} represent the level and index of {\ttfamily left} / {\ttfamily right}, respectively. Level {\ttfamily -\/1} indicates a non-\/existent node.

\mbox{[} {\ttfamily begin}, {\ttfamily end} ) gives the range of indices of mapped values the sub-\/tree rooted at $v$ encompasses, and the sub-\/trees rooted at the left and right child of the current node encompass \mbox{[} {\ttfamily begin}, {\ttfamily div\+\_\+node} ) and \mbox{[} {\ttfamily div\+\_\+node}, {\ttfamily end} ), respectively.
