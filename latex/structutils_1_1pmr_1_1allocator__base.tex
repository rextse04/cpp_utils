struct utils\+::pmr\+::allocator\+\_\+base$<$ T, Traits $>$}
A convenience base class providing typedefs for custom allocators. 

\begin{DoxyRemark}{Remarks}
Due to {\ttfamily std\+::allocator\+\_\+traits}, this is not needed most of the time. However, this can be useful when your allocator depends on a common "{}control type"{} and you would like to synchronize the typedefs across all your allocators. 
\end{DoxyRemark}
