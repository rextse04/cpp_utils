\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
A \href{Trait.html}{\texttt{ {\itshape Meta\+Trait}}} that applies a {\ttfamily Trait} template to all elements of a tuple, producing a single result. 

The result is either a {\ttfamily Type\+Result} or a {\ttfamily Value\+Result} depending on the trait.
